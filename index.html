Tap a group to open it, or tap the body. Count partial- and full-thickness burns only; ignore simple erythema. Age band: adult.

  • The formula gives a starting estimate. Titrate hourly to UOP. ABA 2024 guidance favours starting at the low end (2 mL/kg/%TBSA in adults) to avoid fluid creep.
  • ABLS: adults 2, children <14 y 3, high-voltage electrical 4 mL/kg/%TBSA. Children ≤30 kg also get D5LR maintenance.
  • Resuscitation (LR): 24-h volume = coefficient × weight (kg) × %TBSA. Half goes in the first 8 h from time of burn, half over the next 16 h.
  • Maintenance (D5LR), Holliday-Segar: first 10 kg × 100 mL/kg/day + next 10 kg × 50 mL/kg/day + each kg above 20 × 20 mL/kg/day. Hourly rate = daily volume ÷ 24, run at the same rate for all 24 h. Example: 8 kg → 800 mL/day → 33 mL/h. 25 kg → 1,000 + 500 + 100 = 1,600 mL/day → 67 mL/h.
  • First half is timed from the burn, not from arrival. Catch-up delivers the remaining first-8 h volume over the hours left.
